# Changeling theme asset roles

Use the unthemed files in `public/game-lines/changeling/images/` as geometry references for every Seeming. Theme art changes the visual language, not an asset's job. See `game-lines/changeling/styles/sheet.css` and `app/workspace/sheet-primitives.tsx` for the current rendering geometry.

| Asset | Unthemed size | Required role and constraints |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `attributes-divider` | 2172 × 724 | Transparent, very thin horizontal rule for both sides of the Attributes heading on desktop. CSS places it under the leaf overlay, scales it to the available width, and mirrors the right side. Keep the ornament sparse and its line aligned horizontally; no large central crest. Mobile does not load it. |
| `attributes-divider-leaf` | 1570 × 579 | Transparent one-sided flourish at the inner end of each Attributes heading rule. CSS draws it at 26 px height on desktop and 24 px on mobile, anchored to the right edge and mirrored on the other side. Keep a readable leaf/floral silhouette at that size; it must not become a full two-ended divider. |
| `background-changeling` | 1145 × 1374 | Transparent, centered decorative emblem or watermark. The sheet `::after` renders it once at 50% width and 0.15 opacity on desktop, 72% width and 0.17 opacity on mobile. Keep the motif centered, visually balanced, and isolated from all four edges. Do not draw a page border, parchment, or full-page scene. |
| `column-divider` | 13 × 880 | Transparent hairline vertical column separator. CSS repeats it down the left edge of later trait columns at only 3 px width. Keep it nearly continuous, narrow, and even; wide ornaments disappear or cause visible repetition seams. |
| `experience-purchase-icon` | 84 × 96 | Transparent compact glyph inside a 21 × 21 px Experience purchase control. Use a simple high-contrast silhouette with generous margins, not a detailed illustration requiring a large display size. |
| `frame-center` | 232 × 314 | Compact, transparent central crest for the outer frame. CSS places it in a 44 × 64 px box at 50% background size on desktop, then rotates the same art 180° at the bottom. It must read at roughly 22 px wide and pair with the fine horizontal rails. Do not use a long vertical pendant. |
| `frame-corner` | 1254 × 1254 | Transparent upper-left L-shaped botanical/jewel corner for the outer page border. CSS displays it around 190 × 178 px on desktop, mirrors it to the right, and flips it vertically for bottom corners. Keep useful art near the top and left edges, open center, and a silhouette that survives mirroring. |
| `frame-side` | 120 × 103 | Tiny transparent side accent flanking the center crest on the outer frame. Default CSS shows it near 11 × 24 px; Fairest Desktop uses 18 px width and a 32 px offset from center. It is mirrored left/right and rotated at bottom. Make one compact leaf, gem, or finial with clear margins. Never draw a long rail: CSS already draws the rails, and a wide image is crushed into this tiny box. |
| `paper-texture` | 1254 × 1254 | Opaque subtle paper surface repeated across the sheet at 1024 × 1024 px. It sits beneath all readable text. Keep contrast low, avoid conspicuous objects and obvious tile edges. |
| `section-divider` | 514 × 403 | Transparent compact, one-sided terminal flourish that sits at the left edge of each heading rule and under both sides of the title; CSS mirrors the right side. | `skill-highlight-left` | 760 × 552 | Transparent left end cap of the Kith-skill name frame. CSS scales it to about 52 × 38 px. Open end faces right; the top and bottom rails must reach the right image boundary. Keep its middle clear for text. |

| `tab-texture` | 1280 × 320 | Opaque, dark, low-contrast fill for the **active** tab only. CSS places it behind light text in an inset pseudo-element using `background-size: cover`; the same texture must work in variable-width desktop and mobile tabs. Keep the entire surface dark enough for light text, with no frame, large ornament, bright central patch, or legible writing. Subtle repeatable-looking material texture is appropriate. |
| `title` | 998 × 190 | Transparent wide wordmark in the sheet header. CSS uses `background-size: contain` inside a 998:190 aspect-ratio mark, usually around 380 px wide. For each themed version, spell the **Seeming name**, never “Changeling”; keep the complete word legible at its rendered width with broad horizontal composition. |

The four skill pieces form one uninterrupted horizontal frame behind a variable-width skill name. `KithSkillName` measures the name, inserts alternating middle segments, and leaves the artwork behind the text. Test the assembled frame at actual display size and with short and long names, not only as four enlarged images. The CSS background size is 100% × 100%, so inconsistent aspect ratios, rail positions, or opaque backgrounds are visible immediately. Keep the central text area empty and keep ornamental projections clear of neighboring skill rows.

Before proposing a new theme, assemble its pieces at their CSS display sizes over representative sheet text. Check that the Attributes rule and leaf do not touch the three category labels; that the compact `section-divider` ends the heading rule instead of crossing its title; and that `frame-side` reads as a small finial on each side of `frame-center`. Inspect Desktop and Mobile header title alignment, the active tab texture, and the Kith skill highlight. Theme previews stay separate from active WebP assets until the user approves them.

For Fairest, use luxurious Art Nouveau and Belle Époque jewelry, chiefly aged gold, sage green, blush pink, pearls and pink ivory. Apply each other Seeming's user-specified art style and palette without changing these geometry constraints. Avoid photorealism and hyperrealism.

Elemental uses **period Art Nouveau color lithography**, with flat muted inks, hand-drawn outlines, flowing curves and subtle print grain. Alchemical diagrams and minerals are motifs within that illustration style; glossy 3D gemstones or polished digital fantasy rendering are wrong even when the palette is correct. The palette is petroleum blue, mineral turquoise, aged copper and pale gold. Its wordmark reads `ELEMENTAL`.

For Elemental, the simple four-point alchemical star belongs in `frame-center`; the more detailed mineral crest belongs in `experience-purchase-icon`. Its `frame-side` needs enough display width to read as a finial beside the star. The Elemental `attributes-divider-leaf` orientation is reversed with theme-only CSS while keeping the flourish at the inner end of each heading rule. Its four skill pieces require matching top and bottom rail heights after the theme-specific CSS scaling; verify the complete short-name frame at display size.

Fairest keeps its existing roses, sage leaves, pearls, jeweled motifs and composition. When revising the finish, match a turn-of-the-century illustrated print: matte colors and drawn contours, with restrained gem highlights. Preserve each asset's geometry and transparency so the approved CSS placement still works.
